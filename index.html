How it Works

This is a reverse-Pomodoro timer designed around your actual work flow:

  • Step 1: Choose your rest ratio from the dropdown menu.
  • Step 2: Hit Start and begin your work session. The clock will count up.
  • Step 3: When you need a break, click Stop & Rest.
  • Step 4: The app instantly calculates your earned break time and begins counting down!
